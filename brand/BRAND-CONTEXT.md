# AikyaThiran — brand context (from the planning chat)

## Positioning
- **Brand / domain: AikyaThiran — aikyathiran.in.** Meaning: "skills, together". The name starts with "AI".
- A global, English-only product for students. Style reference: Notion / Slack / Canva.
  Inspirations: Sarvam, Zoho, upGrad, GeeksforGeeks, W3Schools.

## Name decisions
- Earlier working names: Thiran Labs, then ThiranAI.
- Rejected: ThiramAI (Thiramai Inc in Silicon Valley owns thiramai.com, same software/productivity field;
  Thiramai LLP in Chennai).
- Other companies using "Thiran": Thiran Technologies Pvt Ltd, Thiran Softlabs, Thiran Solutions, a government
  "THIRAN" school scheme, and the THIRAN NUTPAM trademark (Class 41). Aikyam Intelligence Pvt Ltd also exists.
- The legal company name can be different from the brand (like Sarvam AI = Axonwise Pvt Ltd). Not decided yet.
- Get a trademark lawyer check before filing "AikyaThiran" (file the full word).

## Products (under AikyaThiran)
- **Thiran Skills** — skill tracks / courses with progress.
- **Thiran Paath** — study app: AI study helper, notes, planner (owner has an older repo "PaathAI").
- **Aikyam** — student community, groups, discussions.
- **Thiran Enterprise** — later, for colleges and companies.

## Logo
- **Chosen: "Sunrise T"** — bold teal T crossing a yellow rising sun. Simple, works at 16px and in one color.
  Files: `brand/logo/sunrise-T/`. The `logo*.svg` files there still say "ThiranAI" in the wordmark; the web app uses
  the "AikyaThiran" wordmark from `frontend/src/components/Logo.tsx`. Make new AikyaThiran wordmark files when needed.
- Earlier detailed version (kept for reference only): `brand/logo/other-options/`.
- Sub-brand idea: same mark plus a small symbol (e.g. `</>` for Thiran Skills, a book for Thiran Paath).

## Colors and type
- Teal `#025049`, sun `#FEB803`, ink `#12221F`, paper `#F4F8F7`. Fonts: Sora (display), Manrope (body).
